# Code of Conduct

Kanvas should be a place where people can contribute without unnecessary hostility or disruption.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- communicate clearly and respectfully
- discuss the code, design, or behavior instead of attacking people
- give useful technical feedback when reviewing changes
- accept that reasonable people can disagree on implementation choices
- keep issue and merge-request discussions relevant to the project
- respect maintainers' decisions about project scope and compatibility requirements

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, or targeted abuse
- discriminatory or demeaning comments
- repeated personal attacks
- deliberate disruption of issues or merge requests
- publishing someone else's private information
- intentionally submitting malicious code or misleading test results

Strong technical disagreement is fine. Personal abuse is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove disruptive content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.

If a conduct issue should not be handled publicly and no private contact channel is published, open a minimal issue asking for a maintainer to establish private contact. Do not include sensitive details in that issue.
